# Examples

This collection of examples is designed to provide hands-on
experience and insights into the integration of Apache Airflow and PySpark. Dive into the following scenarios to enhance
your understanding and proficiency:

## [1. Bill of Lading (BOL)](example_BOL):

In this example, you will find a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) that demonstrates the process of data related to Bill of
Lading (BOL). The DAG performs various tasks, such as data extraction, transformation, and loading. This example serves
as a practical exercise for working with BOL data within the context of Apache Airflow.

## [2. Open Sanctions](example_opensanctions)

Explore the capabilities of Apache Airflow, Pyspark with this example focusing on Open Sanctions data. Open Sanctions
provides a wealth of information about individuals and entities involved in global sanctions, corruption, and other
illicit activities.
